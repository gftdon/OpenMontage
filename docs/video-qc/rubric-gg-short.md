## 2. Required evidence and coverage

Read and view the rendered artifact yourself. Local ffprobe measures stream inventory, duration, display dimensions and frame timestamp cadence. Local ffmpeg performs full video+audio decode, full-length `ebur128=peak=true`, `silencedetect`, `blackdetect`, `freezedetect`. Preserve commands, tool versions, thresholds, exit status, summary values and logs. Default detector settings (leads, not automatic verdicts): `silencedetect=noise=-40dB:d=0.2`, `blackdetect=d=0.1:pix_th=0.10:pic_th=0.98`, `freezedetect=n=-60dB:d=0.5`. These trigger sensitivity settings do not define acceptable content loss. Record actual settings if changed for further investigation; retain the initial whole-file pass. A successful empty event list is not a missing log. Nonfinite loudness or unparseable summary is unavailable evidence, never a passing measurement.

Inspect frames at 2 fps in the opening 0..3 seconds, at least 1 fps thereafter including the last decodable frame, plus before/at/after cuts, text changes, suspicious events and defects. Inspect all extracted contact-sheet frames and use full-resolution crops/adjacent frames for text and anatomy judgments. Keep timecodes in seconds from presentation start and original timestamp correspondence. Sampling does not prove every frame is defect-free: disclose inspected timestamps/intervals, sampling rate, gaps and remaining coverage limits. Normal declared sampling is not by itself an incomplete review; inability to perform the required sample/content inspection is. View every text appearance identified during local playback; if a brief text interval is found, sample its start/middle/end rather than relying on the 1 fps grid.

### Objective audio procedure (no subjective listening)

Audio score is based solely on actual local measurements and personally inspected images. Preserve full-length ebur128 integrated LUFS, true peak and LRA (LU); run full-length astats for per-channel/overall sample peak, RMS, peak counts/flat-topped-run clipping indications and DC offset. Keep raw logs, timecoded windows, tool versions/settings/exits and derived values. LRA, DC and clipping indicators have **no extra numeric pass/critical thresholds**: record suspected anomalies for inspection, not an invented signal-quality verdict. Only the locked integrated -15..-13 LUFS and true peak <= -1 dBTP are numeric audio gates.

Generate BOTH local `showwavespic` and `showspectrumpic` images covering the entire audio and enlarged final 1.2 seconds. Personally inspect them with Read; record generator, settings, time range, artifact path, evidence ID and personally_inspected true. A generated image without personal inspection is insufficient. Waveform/spectrum indications are leads, not proof of perceived noise, intelligibility, perceptual sync or music quality. Explicitly report no subjective listening; do not claim these perceptual properties were verified. This declared limitation alone does not prevent PASS. Inability to inspect images, absent stream, missing/failed/unparseable required measurement or unresolved objective check => incomplete FAIL. Preserve already verified facts/criticals.

**Duration alignment:** compare decoded first/last presentation timestamps plus sample/frame durations, after applying declared encoder delay/padding/edit offsets. Record audio_start_s/audio_end_s, video_start_s/video_end_s, start/end deltas, sample rate, audio-frame duration and tolerance. The quantization tolerance is max(1/30 s, one decoded audio-frame duration), derived from actual decoded timing, not an arbitrary creative allowance. Record both raw and adjusted endpoints. A discrepancy outside this tolerance requires local timestamp/window investigation. Once confirmed after encoder padding/delay adjustment, duration mismatch is ALWAYS a SPEC critical, regardless of perceived impact. Record status confirmed_mismatch and endpoint evidence. Unconfirmed/unclear alignment gets an issue plus limitations and incomplete FAIL; never silently PASS. A raw rounding/padding discrepancy is not confirmed until adjusted timing has been verified.

**Ending fade:** measure [T-1.2,T] as twelve consecutive 100 ms RMS/peak windows, plus [T-0.020,T] final 20 ms RMS/peak and last decoded sample amplitude (per channel), T = adjusted decoded audio end. Record exact bounds/sample counts and levels; digital zero may be represented as null dB with an explicit digital_zero flag, not a JSON infinity. Inspect enlarged waveform/spectrum of the same tail. Compare first/last envelope and intermediate windows allowing music transients: require a supported decay toward the observed terminal floor, not active-level truncation at the file boundary. No universal dB floor or strictly monotonic envelope is assumed, and a natural decay may establish the fade without proving an authored filter. The editing skill promises a 1.2-second fade-out. Record status verified_decay, confirmed_missing_fade or unverified and rationale. Confirmed absence of that ending fade (including active-level abrupt cutoff) is ALWAYS a SPEC critical, regardless of perceived impact. Missing/unclear measurement is not proof of absent fade: add issue plus limitations and incomplete FAIL, never silently PASS. A silent tail alone does not prove fade: inspect its entry and preceding envelope. No subjective proof may be invented.

Set coverage.objective_audio_review true only when all mandatory measurements, timecoded detector dispositions, personally inspected images and duration/fade review are complete; full_audio_review remains false. Keep perceptual limitations in coverage.unverified but distinguish them from missing **required objective** evidence. Verify visual content yourself rather than relying solely on tool/model output.

## 3. Critical gates (verified evidence required)

Every critical has a stable code, description, start_s/end_s (whole-file range for global measurements), evidence_ids, and observed vs expected facts. Unknown/suspected findings are not promoted to verified criticals; a material unresolved check instead produces incomplete FAIL.

| Code | Critical condition |
|---|---|
| SPEC | Wrong final display size/aspect, rotation/SAR yielding other than 1080x1920; not constant 30 fps (30000/1001 is not 30); duration outside 10..90 seconds inclusive; not exactly one playable video and one playable audio stream; corrupt/unplayable stream or destructive decode failure. No added codec gate. Verify A/V endpoints, not just container labels. Confirmed adjusted duration mismatch beyond max(1/30 s, one decoded audio-frame duration) and confirmed missing promised 1.2-second ending fade are ALWAYS SPEC criticals, not conditional on content destruction. |
| AI_INCONSISTENCY | Visible verified malformed anatomy or face/identity inconsistency, confirmed from rendered frames and temporal context. Intentional, coherent stylization is not presumed an artifact. |
| TEXT_READABILITY | Any viewer-visible text unreadable at normal 1080x1920 playback because of clipping, contrast, overlap, missing glyphs, size or insufficient hold. Cite crop/interval and exact obstruction, not font preference. |
| SAFE_ZONE | Viewer-visible text bounds outside x=90..990 or y=210..1600. Measure bounding box in original display pixels; boundaries inclusive, all visible glyphs/effects must fit. A background image is not text. |
| NON_ENGLISH_TEXT | Any non-English viewer-visible text anywhere, including text baked into footage, labels, captions and signs. Non-Latin letters are critical. Latin non-English text is also critical (e.g. Spanish “Hola mundo”); a Latin-only check is necessary but not sufficient. Unicode letters outside Script=Latin offend; numbers and punctuation are not letters. Latin diacritics alone do not prove non-English: inspect meaning/context, distinguish English proper names and technical identifiers, document uncertainty and fail incomplete when material language cannot be verified. Do not apply this gate to spoken language alone. |
| COPYING | Proven copying of a supplied do_not_copy string's distinctive phrase, ordered sequence/beat or distinctive visual concept. Cite supplied index/value, rendered match, timecode and why distinctive. A shared generic topic/style is insufficient. |
| LOUDNESS | Measured final integrated loudness outside -14 LUFS ±1, i.e. below -15 or above -13 LUFS. Boundaries inclusive. |
| TRUE_PEAK | Measured final true peak > -1 dBTP (not sample peak). Exactly -1 passes this gate. |
| CONTENT_BROKEN | Broken audio, black or freeze that destroys intended content, confirmed with context and impact. Include timecode and what the viewer loses. Detector flags alone do not qualify. |

Short intentional silence is not automatic critical. There is no blanket 0.5-second silence rule (nor a detector duration as a critical threshold). A deliberate pause, black transition or coherent still hold may be fine; assess objective impact with nearby frames, timecoded measurements and inspected audio images; do not infer perceived sound. A short destructive dropout can still be critical. Record rationale either way.

## 4. Reproducible anchored scoring (100 points)

No per-category floors. Score all seven categories even on a verified critical failure if evidence is otherwise complete. A critical never becomes acceptable because a category scores highly. Scores support fractions, not forced integers.

Each category has two equal-weight checks. Rate each check from 0..4 using the anchors below: 0 = absent/broken, 1 = substantial repeated defects, 2 = functional but ordinary or materially uneven, 3 = strong with only localized minor defects, 4 = consistently excellent with affirmative evidence. For each row, 0/2/4 define content-specific anchors; 1 and 3 sit between adjacent anchors only when the intermediate conditions are evidenced. Choose the highest fully supported anchor, then optionally interpolate between adjacent anchors in 0.1-level increments: `level = lower + 0.1*k`, k=0..9. Document both adjacent anchors, the concrete observed coverage/defect differences, and why k is justified. Do not interpolate without observations or inflate a borderline pass. Score = `weight * (checkA_level + checkB_level) / 8`; total = sum of seven category scores, retain exact fractions for verdict (presentation may separately display rounded values). Same fact may inform multiple distinct checks; do not add arbitrary deductions outside this formula.

| Category/max | Check A (0 / 2 / 4) | Check B (0 / 2 / 4) |
|---|---|---|
| hook / 20 | Opening clarity in first 3s: no understandable focal event / discernible setup but delayed / immediate specific focal event and premise readable by 3s | Curiosity/payoff promise: no reason to continue / generic curiosity / distinctive unresolved question or reveal that the ending actually pays off |
| visual / 25 | Anatomy and identity continuity: incoherent/broken / mostly coherent with visible distractions / convincing consistent anatomy, face, objects and spatial relationships across inspected shots | Composition and motion: obscured subject or arbitrary motion / competent framing with uneven transitions / intentional focus, controlled movement and clean scene-to-scene continuity throughout inspected material |
| text / 15 | Legibility/layout: unreadable / readable with minor spacing/hold issues / all observed text comfortably readable, unclipped, within safe zone at normal playback | English and editorial precision: meaning obscured/non-English / English but verbose or generic / concise correct English supporting each beat without redundancy; if genuinely no visible text, full marks only after verifying absence in required coverage |
| story_pacing / 15 | Progression/payoff: disconnected/no ending / clear beginning-middle-end but weak escalation / every beat advances an identifiable arc to earned payoff | Rhythm: destructive dead time or rushed meaning / serviceable with localized drag / deliberate shot/text timing, motivated pauses and clean satisfying close, evidenced by a timecoded beat map |
| audio / 10 | Objective levels/integrity: broken or grossly noncompliant / compliant LUFS/true peak with localized measured anomalies explained / compliant levels with full LRA/astats logs and inspected waveform/spectrum, no unresolved objective anomalies; no inferred perceptual quality | Objective continuity/end: destructive measured loss / verified alignment/fade with localized non-destructive irregularity / timestamp alignment, timecoded silence dispositions and supported ending decay, corroborated by both inspected image types; no claim of perceptual sync |
| originality / 10 | Supplied exclusion comparison: proven distinctive copying / no proven copy but generic treatment / visibly distinct concept, ordered beats and language compared with every supplied string | Own creative specificity: copied/generic assembly without purpose / competent familiar treatment / specific visual/narrative choices coherently serving this Short; do not claim global novelty |
| technical / 5 | Delivered format: wrong/unplayable / correct basic specs but localized non-destructive cadence/endpoint concern / all specs, timestamp cadence and endpoints measured cleanly | Integrity: content destroyed or corrupt / successful full decode with localized noncritical detector findings / full decode clean and every detector event explained with frame/measurement/image evidence |

Provide a timecoded beat map and text inventory as evidence, not just adjectives. For each check, record check name, level, maximum, points, rationale and evidence IDs in scoring_details. A no-text decision and an absence-of-event decision need coverage evidence, not an assumption. Originality is limited to supplied strings and inspected final content; no source search. Record status `no_observed_match`, `verified_copying`, `uncertain` or `unverified`. `uncertain` is not proven copying; unresolved material uncertainty is incomplete FAIL. No observed match is not proof of worldwide originality.

## 5. Verdict and incomplete results

`PASS` iff scoring_status is `complete`, total > 90, and criticals is empty. Exactly 90 = FAIL; 90.5 with empty criticals = PASS; 99 with any verified critical = FAIL. No per-category minimum, rounding shortcut or alternate pass label.

Missing/invalid input, tool failure (including timeout/unparseable required measurement), inaccessible frames, missing required objective audio evidence or any other required material evidence => `scoring_status: "incomplete"`, `total: 0`, `verdict: "FAIL"`, and all seven category values null. Here 0 is an **unscored sentinel**, not a measured quality score or a sum of partial checks. The config's `incomplete.category_scores: null` describes each category value; the report category_scores object retains all seven keys. Do not fabricate scores, event arrays, measurements or evidence. Use measurements null when nothing was measured; for partially measured results preserve verified values and use null for unavailable individual values, explaining coverage. Keep proven criticals even on incomplete reports. If total is genuinely scored zero with complete evidence, status is complete, distinguishable from sentinel zero.
